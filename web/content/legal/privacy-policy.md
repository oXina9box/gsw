## 2. IMPORTANT NOTICE: NO HIPAA / NO PHI

Gem Studio is not designed for healthcare use and is not intended to create, receive, maintain, transmit, or process Protected Health Information (“PHI”) under HIPAA or similar health privacy laws.

Gem Studio is not a covered entity or business associate under HIPAA. The Service is not HIPAA-compliant, and we do not provide a Business Associate Agreement (“BAA”).

Do not submit any health-related information to the Service, including but not limited to:

- Medical records
- Diagnoses or treatment information
- Health conditions
- Insurance or claims data
- Genetic or biometric health data
- Prescription information
- Mental health information
- Any other PHI or sensitive health information

If you are a healthcare provider, health plan, clearinghouse, or any person or entity subject to HIPAA or similar laws, do not use the Service for regulated health-related purposes.

If you accidentally submit PHI, please remove it immediately and contact us at hello@gemstudio.app.

## 4. THIRD-PARTY AI AND CLOUD PROCESSING

Gem Studio currently does not operate its own artificial intelligence models. AI-assisted features rely on third-party cloud and AI providers.

When you use AI features, your inputs, prompts, project context, uploaded materials, or related data may be transmitted to third-party providers for processing.

These providers may include:

- AI model or API providers
- Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers
- Authentication and database providers, such as Supabase
- Storage, media processing, or rendering providers
- Analytics and monitoring providers
- Payment processors
- Customer support tools, if used

We share data with third-party providers only to the extent necessary to provide, secure, support, or improve the Service, subject to the applicable provider terms and configurations.

These providers may have their own privacy policies, terms of service, data retention practices, and content policies. Where available, we may use enterprise controls, configuration settings, or contractual protections, but we cannot control every aspect of third-party processing.

## 9. DATA RETENTION

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and according to our documented retention schedule.

Typical retention approach:

- Account data: retained while your account is active.
- Project content: retained while the relevant project or workspace exists.
- AI prompts and outputs: retained as part of your project history unless deleted.
